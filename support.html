Before emailing

Include your macOS version, QuitBuddy version, the affected app, and the quit mode you configured. Screenshots are helpful when they do not reveal private data.

Red close button quit

If the red window button does not quit an app like ⌘Q, mention whether the app uses standard macOS traffic-light buttons, a custom title bar, or full-screen windows.

Licenses and refunds

Standard licenses are managed through Lemon Squeezy. For refunds, email within 14 days of purchase and include the purchase email or order reference.

Direct contact

Email quitbuddy.support@gmail.com. Please do not send license keys unless specifically requested.
