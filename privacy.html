Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

QuitBuddy is designed to keep quit protection local, predictable, and easy to understand. This policy explains how the macOS app and static website handle personal information.

No analytics SDKs The app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
No typed content recording Input Monitoring is used only to detect configured quit actions.
Local by default Rules, preferences, diagnostics, and exports stay on your Mac.

Data Collection

QuitBuddy does not collect analytics, transmit keyboard input, sell data, or store personal usage data. Settings, protected-app rules, quit modes, and diagnostics remain on your Mac and never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

Permissions

  • Input Monitoring lets QuitBuddy detect Command-Q and, when you enable the Standard close-button setting, detect clicks on a standard window's red close button so it can quit the owning app like Command-Q. The app does not record typed content, collect keystroke or click history, analyze what you type, or upload keyboard input.
  • Accessibility lets QuitBuddy return focus and send a confirmed quit action to the intended app after you explicitly choose to quit. It is used only for configured quit behavior.

These permissions are optional but required for QuitBuddy's core protection features. You can revoke them at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Purchases

The first public direct-download release uses Lemon Squeezy for checkout, payment processing, license-key delivery, and license validation. QuitBuddy does not receive or store credit card details.

In direct-download builds, the app contacts Lemon Squeezy's public license endpoints when you activate, validate, or deactivate a license. The app stores the license key and minimal license metadata in your Mac's Keychain so Standard access can be restored locally. QuitBuddy does not store your Lemon Squeezy customer email in its local license record.

Free And Standard

Free mode works without an account and protects one active app rule. Standard unlocks unlimited per-app rules and the optional setting that makes standard red window close buttons quit apps like Command-Q. License checks are limited to activation, validation, deactivation, and restoring saved license status.

App Updates

Direct-download builds may periodically contact getquitbuddy.com to fetch static update metadata and may use Sparkle to fetch an appcast and download a signed update archive when you choose to update. QuitBuddy compares update metadata with the installed app version locally. The request does not include your installed version, license key, protected apps, rules, settings, quit history, or any unique device identifier. Standard web server logs may still record request metadata such as IP address, timestamp, user agent, and requested path.

Third-Party Services

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash reporters. Lemon Squeezy is used for direct checkout and license management. EmailOctopus is used only for optional update emails if you explicitly join the QuitBuddy update list. The website is static and may collect standard server logs through the hosting provider.

Website

The website uses privacy-friendly aggregate analytics to measure page views and conversion events such as download and checkout clicks. These website analytics do not use tracking cookies, create cross-site ad profiles, or add app telemetry. External links, such as Lemon Squeezy checkout or download hosting, are governed by those providers' own policies once you leave the QuitBuddy site.

Optional Update Emails

After starting a free download, you may optionally enter your email address to receive QuitBuddy release notes, setup tips, and occasional product updates. The download does not require an email address.

If you join the update list, the website sends your email address to a small serverless endpoint that forwards it to EmailOctopus. QuitBuddy does not store the email address in the app, in a local website database, or in the Git repository. You can unsubscribe from update emails at any time.

Children

QuitBuddy is suitable for general audiences. The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children, and it contains no advertising or web browsing. The optional website update list is not intended for children.

Contact

For privacy questions, email quitbuddy.support@gmail.com.
